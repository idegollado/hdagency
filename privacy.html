Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026 · Versión en español

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Juan Issac Hernandez (HDTech) ("HDTech", "we", "us") is a personal branding agency based in Monterrey, Nuevo León, Mexico. We are the party responsible (responsable) for the personal data described in this policy.

This policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal data when you:

  • hire our personal branding services;
  • connect your LinkedIn account to HDTech Account Manager, our internal reporting application; or
  • contact us or visit this website.

Privacy contact: issac.degollado@gmail.com

2. Personal data we collect

Data you give us directly

  • Identification and contact data: name, email address, phone number, company and job title.
  • Professional information you share for the brand diagnostic: work history, goals, target industries and audiences.
  • Billing information, when applicable.

Data from LinkedIn, only with your explicit authorization

When you connect your LinkedIn account to HDTech Account Manager through LinkedIn's official sign-in (OAuth 2.0), and only for the permissions you approve on LinkedIn's consent screen, we receive:

  • Basic profile data: name, profile photo, headline and LinkedIn member ID.
  • Analytics for your own posts: impressions, members reached, reactions, comments and reposts.
  • Your follower count and follower growth.
  • Identifiers of posts published through our application, so we can match them to their analytics.

We never receive your LinkedIn password. We do not collect data about other LinkedIn members, such as your connections, the people who engage with your posts, or their profiles.

Technical data

  • Access tokens issued by LinkedIn, stored encrypted.
  • Basic logs, such as access dates and errors, used for security and troubleshooting.

We do not collect sensitive personal data.

3. How we use your data

We use your personal data only to:

  1. Provide the personal branding services you hired, including your diagnostic, strategy and content plan.
  2. Measure the performance of your own LinkedIn presence and prepare private reports for you, compared against the goals we agreed together.
  3. Publish content to your LinkedIn profile, when you ask us to and approve it.
  4. Communicate with you about the service, and handle billing.
  5. Keep the application secure and meet our legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling for third parties, selling data, or any purpose unrelated to the service you hired.

Legal basis. We process your data based on your consent and on the service agreement between you and HDTech, in accordance with Mexico's Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los Particulares (2025). Connecting your LinkedIn account is optional, and you can withdraw your consent at any time (see section 7). If we ever need to use your data for a new purpose, we will ask for your consent again first.

4. Our commitments on LinkedIn data

We access LinkedIn data only through LinkedIn's official APIs and in accordance with LinkedIn's API Terms of Use. In particular, we:

  • request only the permissions needed for the purposes in section 3;
  • show each client only their own data;
  • do not scrape LinkedIn, automate actions on LinkedIn, or access data about other members;
  • do not sell, rent or license LinkedIn data, and do not combine it with third-party data sources to build profiles;
  • do not use LinkedIn data for advertising or for any purpose you have not authorized;
  • delete LinkedIn data when you revoke access or when our service ends, as described in section 6.

6. Retention, deletion and security

Retention

  • LinkedIn access tokens and analytics: kept while your LinkedIn account is connected and our service is active.
  • When you revoke access or the service ends, we delete your LinkedIn tokens immediately and your LinkedIn data within 30 days.
  • Contract and billing records: kept for the period required by Mexican tax and commercial law.

Security

We apply administrative, technical and physical measures to protect your data, including:

  • encryption of access tokens and data in transit (HTTPS/TLS);
  • access limited to the HDTech team members who serve your account;
  • two-factor authentication on our accounts; and
  • removal of access when a team member or client relationship ends.

If a security incident significantly affects your data, we will notify you without undue delay so you can take steps to protect yourself.

7. Your rights and how to exercise them

You have the right to access your personal data, rectify it, cancel (delete) it, and oppose its use (ARCO rights). You can also withdraw your consent or limit how we use your data.

To exercise any of these rights, email issac.degollado@gmail.com with:

  • your name and a way for us to reply;
  • a document that proves your identity, or your representative's authority; and
  • a clear description of what you are requesting.

We will respond within the time limits set by applicable law.

Disconnecting LinkedIn. You can revoke HDTech Account Manager's access at any time, either by asking us or directly on LinkedIn: Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services, then remove the application.

Data protection authority. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may contact Mexico's data protection authority, the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or legal requirements change. We will publish the updated version on this page with its new effective date, and notify active clients by email of any significant change.
