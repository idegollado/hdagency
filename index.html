HDTech helps professionals build a clear, credible presence on LinkedIn, so the right companies, recruiters and decision makers know who they are and what they bring.

How we work

1. Diagnostic

We review your experience, goals and current LinkedIn baseline to define where your brand should go.

2. Strategy

Positioning, headline, content pillars and the profile of the people you want to reach.

3. Execution and measurement

A weekly content plan, genuine interactions, and private reports on your own LinkedIn performance.

Your data, your control

When a client connects their LinkedIn account to our reporting tool, HDTech Account Manager, they do it through LinkedIn's official sign-in and can revoke access at any time. We only read analytics for the client's own posts and followers, and we never share or sell that data. Read our Privacy Policy.
